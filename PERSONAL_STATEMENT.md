# Personal Statement — Anchal Bisht

**For:** Erasmus+, Türkiye Bursları, UNESCO Youth, International Development Applications
**Project:** KAIROS — AI-Powered Educational Equity Platform
**Date:** 2026

---

I did not choose to build KAIROS. I built it because I had no choice but to.

I am the first person in my entire family to pursue higher education in the real sense of the word. No parent, no relative, no older sibling had walked this road before me. When it was time to choose my career path — the most important decision of my young life — I had no one who truly understood what I was navigating.

I listened to cousins and siblings who were themselves confused. I followed advice that came from fear, not knowledge. I ended up in a field I never wanted — not because I lacked ability, but because I lacked guidance. I had no map. I had no older sister who had already been through it and come back to help.

That experience did not break me. It clarified me.

I began to see that my story was not unique. Across India — in Tier 2 cities, in semi-rural towns, in families where no one had ever filled a college application form — millions of young people were making the same confused, uninformed decisions I made. Not because they were not capable. Because they had no platform, no voice, no guide.

I am not from a wealthy background. I did not have connections to professors or NGOs or international organizations. What I had was the memory of my own confusion — and the conviction that technology, built with empathy and purpose, could be the older sister I never had.

So I built KAIROS.

KAIROS — named for the ancient Greek concept of the sacred, decisive moment — is an AI-powered educational equity platform for first-generation students in India. At its heart is KAI, an emotionally intelligent AI companion designed to feel like a warm, patient older sister. KAI guides students through scholarship discovery, career decisions, and the emotional weight of being the first in their family to take this road.

But KAIROS is not only for first-generation students. It is for every young person who is fighting for themselves — every girl who wants independence, every boy who refuses to give up, every student who thinks more deeply than the world around them and deserves the tools to act on that depth.

My goal was never only to build something for India. The problem of young people being left without guidance, without opportunity, without voice — that is not an Indian problem. That is a human problem. And I want KAIROS to be a human solution.

I graduated in 2025, in a period of confusion and redirection that taught me more than any classroom. I explored different paths — examinations, defense, employment — and found that none of them aligned with what I actually wanted: to speak for people. To help solve problems at the root, not just treat symptoms. To build things that matter to the world, not just to companies.

That search led me to international development. To the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. To the understanding that education equity — SDG 4.5 — and gender equality — SDG 5.1 — are not abstract policy targets. They are the daily reality of millions of young people who simply need someone to show them the door.

KAIROS is my attempt to be that someone. At scale. With technology. With warmth.

I am also building Chianya — a youth mental health companion designed to feel like a forest, a home, a place of quiet consciousness. Because guidance, mental health, and opportunity are not separate problems. They belong together.

I do not want to help people by encouraging dependency. I want to solve problems at the root. I want to build systems that give people the tools to help themselves. That is what KAIROS is. That is what I am committed to.

If given this opportunity, I will bring back not just skills — but a platform already being used by real students, real data on educational inequality, and a proven model for AI-powered equity that can be adapted for other developing regions.

I am ready. KAIROS is ready. The students who need it have been ready for a long time.

# KAIROS — The Sacred Moment

> *Every first-generation student deserves their KAIROS moment — the moment they decide to rise above everything.*

## Theory of Change

**Problem:** First-generation students in Tier 2/3 India lack access to guidance, mentorship, and scholarship information that urban students receive through family networks.

**Activity:** KAIROS platform providing AI mentorship, scholarship discovery, and peer community.

**Output:** Students reached, scholarships discovered, questions answered.

**Outcome:** First-generation students apply to opportunities they would never have found alone.

**Impact:** Reduction in education inequality, contribution to SDG 4.5 and SDG 5.1.

## The Problem at Scale

- **40 million** first-generation college students in India
- **40-50%** of enrolled students are first-generation
- **Girls** from rural/semi-urban areas face dropout rates 2-3x higher
- **₹3,000+ crore** in scholarships go unclaimed every year
- Career counseling costs **₹2,000–₹20,000** per session — inaccessible to those who need it most

---

## The Solution

**KAI** — an emotionally intelligent AI companion modeled as an older sister who already made it through college. Available 24/7. Free forever. Judgment-free.

### Core Features
- 🤖 **KAI Chat** — AI companion powered by Groq (Llama 3), understands English and Hinglish
- 🧭 **Career Compass** — 7-question guided journey to discover realistic career paths
- 🏆 **Scholarships Hub** — 500+ real Indian scholarships with plain-English guidance
- 📖 **Stories Wall** — Real first-generation student stories for peer inspiration
- 🙋 **Ask Anything** — Fully anonymous Q&A, no login required
- 📊 **My KAIROS** — Personal dashboard, application tracker, goal setting

---

## Target Population

**Primary:** Girls aged 17–23, first in their family to attend college, from Tier 2/3 cities and semi-rural India.

**Secondary:** All first-generation college students regardless of gender.

**Geography:** India — 28 states covered in scholarship database, expanding weekly.

---

## Technical Stack

**Frontend:** React 18, Vite, Three.js, Framer Motion, Tailwind CSS

**Backend:** Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, Groq API (Llama 3)

**Security:** JWT httpOnly cookies, bcrypt, rate limiting, input validation

**Infrastructure:** PWA-ready, deployable on Vercel + Render + MongoDB Atlas
